IMTP 用后置摄像头，在大腿中部等长提拉发力之前，查看膝关节、髋关节和躯干角度。测力仍要使用测力台。本应用没有医疗认证，不提供诊断或治疗。

当前版本在本机使用 RTMO-s 与 Apple Vision 2D；这是单镜头二维姿态观察工具，不等同于经标定的关节角测量。

IMTP checks knee, hip, and trunk angles with the rear camera before an isometric mid-thigh pull. Bar force still requires a force plate. This app has no medical certification and does not diagnose or treat.

The current version uses RTMO-s and Apple Vision 2D on device. Single-camera 2D posture estimates do not replace calibrated angle measurement.
