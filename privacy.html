隐私政策

IMTP · 生效日期 2026年10月20日

本政策说明 IMTP（开发者 Guanhua Li）如何处理信息。应用不设账号。

摄像头

后置摄像头只用于在本机实时计算膝关节、髋关节和躯干角度。画面不保存，也不由本应用上传。

运动传感器

加速度计只用来判断手机哪一边朝上，从而把躯干角度对齐到垂直面。这些读数只在本机使用，不保存，也不上传。

保存在这台设备上的角度

你点「记下角度」之后，最近最多 8 组数字（时间、膝、髋、躯干、侧面）会存在这台设备上。这些数字不上传。删除应用即可清除。

姿态识别组件

关节位置由设备上的姿态识别组件计算。该组件的隐私清单声明：它可能为分析和功能收集设备标识、诊断数据、性能数据、产品交互和其他数据。这些数据不与你的身份关联，也不用于跨应用追踪。本应用不会把画面或角度交给该用途。

不会做的事

本应用不出售个人信息，不投放广告，也不做跨应用追踪。

Privacy Policy

Effective 20 October 2026. IMTP, developed by Guanhua Li, has no account.

The rear camera is used only to measure knee, hip, and trunk angles on this device. Frames are not saved and are not uploaded by this app.

Motion data is used only to tell which way the phone is held, so trunk angle can be compared with vertical. It is not saved or uploaded.

If you tap the record control, up to eight sets of numbers (time, knee, hip, trunk, side) stay on this device. They are not uploaded. Deleting the app removes them.

A pose component on the device finds the joints. Its privacy manifest says it may collect a device identifier, diagnostics, performance data, product interaction, and other data for analytics and functionality. That data is not linked to your identity and is not used for tracking. This app does not send frames or angles for that purpose.

The app does not sell personal information, show ads, or track you across apps.
